## Decision

No production change in this cycle. Three independent safeguards argue for waiting:

1. Search zero-click candidates remain below 200 impressions.
2. Site-to-YouTube conversion remains below 20 attributed clicks.
3. YouTube is actively A/B testing the underperforming `WHITE SHIRTS AT BORODINO` packaging.

Next cycle should specifically check:

- the completed White Shirts title test and its winning watch-time share;
- whether `/songs/shiloh-ballad` crosses 200 zero-click impressions;
- whether attributed `youtube_click` reaches 20 and reveals a source winner;
- the seven-day profile of `Roger Lamb: Saratoga POW`;
- whether any external Voice or `voice_submit` event appears.
