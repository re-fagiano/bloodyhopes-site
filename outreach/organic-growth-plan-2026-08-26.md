# Bloody Hopes organic growth plan

Date established: 26 August 2026  
Measurement window: 14–21 days per material change

## Objective

Turn existing search visibility into qualified visits, then lead readers naturally from a historical question to a related Bloody Hopes song. Growth must come from useful, sourced material rather than mass-produced pages, hidden keywords, bought links, or unrelated promotion.

## Baseline

Search Console, 4–24 August 2026:

- Site total: 402 impressions, 68 clicks, 16.9% CTR, average position 13.
- Shiloh ballad: 81 impressions, 0 clicks, average position 6.9.
- From Lance to Repeater: 68 impressions, 0 clicks, average position 11.2.
- Seeing the Elephant: 22 impressions, 0 clicks, average position 14.7.
- Old Ironsides: 45 impressions, 2 clicks, average position 30.3.
- Austerlitz Sun: 5 impressions, 1 click, average position 5.8.

The strongest disclosed Shiloh queries concern M. Ebenezer Wescott and the 17th Wisconsin. The disclosed cavalry queries concern cavalry weapons, guns, and pistol cavalry.

## Guardrails

- Preserve canonical URLs, source citations, lyrics, historical qualifications, and the existing visual identity.
- Never create hidden keyword blocks or near-duplicate pages.
- Do not change or contact the YouTube channel during this cycle.
- Make one evidence-based batch at a time; run the complete test suite before publishing.
- Do not judge a search change before 14 days unless it causes a technical failure.
- Keep a dated baseline so improvements can be reversed if clicks, CTR, or rankings deteriorate materially.

## Workstreams

### 1. Capture existing demand

- Align Shiloh's search title and historical heading with Wescott and the 17th Wisconsin without disguising that the page contains a ballad.
- Align the cavalry article's title and description with the demonstrated “cavalry weapons” intent.
- Review CTR, impressions, and average position after 14–21 days.

Success signal: at least one organic click on each priority page, with no sustained loss of impressions.

### 2. Build three coherent topic clusters

- Civil War eyewitnesses: Shiloh, Seeing the Elephant, Gettysburg, Wescott.
- Cavalry and military change: From Lance to Repeater, Light Brigade, Austerlitz, Waterloo.
- Naval history and folklore: Old Ironsides, USS Constitution, CSS Alabama.

Every central article should link to its related songs, and each song should return a contextual link to the relevant article. Links must be editorially useful, not repeated sitewide boilerplate.

Success signal: growth in visits that open a second related page or proceed to YouTube.

### 3. Create one citation-worthy asset

Preferred first asset: a concise annotated guide to M. Ebenezer Wescott's 13 April 1862 Shiloh letter, clearly separating transcription, context, inference, and the ballad's fictional treatment.

Alternative asset: an illustrated timeline of cavalry weapons based on the existing sourced article.

Do not start both at once. Select the asset supported by the strongest new Search Console demand after the first measurement window.

Success signal: new non-brand queries, referral visits, or earned references from relevant history pages and communities.

### 4. Ethical distribution

- Share only material that directly answers an existing discussion or research need.
- Lead with the primary source, historical finding, or visual asset—not a request to stream the music.
- Avoid repetitive submissions, automated outreach, link exchanges, and communities that prohibit self-promotion.
- Record the destination, date, shared asset, and resulting referral traffic.

### 5. Weekly measurement

Each review records:

- impressions, clicks, CTR, and position for the five priority pages;
- newly disclosed queries;
- indexed page count and sitemap health;
- site page views, content opens, and YouTube clicks;
- any new referrals or backlinks;
- MCP research reads and accepted contributions, tracked separately from human search traffic.

## Decision rules

- High impressions, low CTR, position 1–10: improve the snippet and intent match.
- Position 11–20: strengthen useful content and contextual internal links.
- No impressions after 6–8 weeks: stop expanding the page until demand or distribution evidence appears.
- Rising clicks: preserve the page and avoid unnecessary rewrites.
- Traffic without downstream engagement: improve the transition from history to song rather than adding more traffic sources.
