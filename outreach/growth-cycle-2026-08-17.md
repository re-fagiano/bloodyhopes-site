## Decision

Do not create a new article, alter titles, or deploy a conversion redesign in this cycle. Preserve the current implementation and collect a comparable snapshot next cycle. Reconsider only when one of these conditions is met:

- Search Console reaches at least 200 impressions for a page/query pair;
- a middle-funnel event records at least 20 observations;
- YouTube exposes a repeatable content-level CTR or retention outlier;
- the Founding Archive gains a new external Voice.
