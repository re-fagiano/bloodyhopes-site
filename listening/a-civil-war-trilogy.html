April 9 is not April 12

The National Park Service distinguishes Lee’s surrender agreement on April 9 from the formal infantry ceremony on April 12. Appomattox was a decisive surrender of the Army of Northern Virginia; other Confederate forces remained in the field. The compilation’s language of “the end of the war” is a narrative frame, not a claim that all fighting ceased that afternoon.

Empathy and political purpose

Listening to an imagined Confederate soldier does not settle the justice of secession. The sequence puts private grief beside political consequences and the unfinished demands of emancipation. Its perspectives are deliberately unequal in what they know and what they can justify. Read the causes study alongside the surrender narrative to distinguish a character’s belief from the project’s historical account.
